Question 9 · Moderate

Solarpunk is an art movement that imagines renewable energy–powered technology infused complementarily into nature. In Paolo Bacigalupi’s solarpunk short story “Efficiency,” an artificial intelligence that absorbs sustainable energies, redistributing them through intricate networks of weights and generators, ______ Chicago’s energy grid.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

Student answer: C · Correct answer: C · Correct

Correct · Correct answer: C

Show explanation

Bracket “that absorbs sustainable energies” and “redistributing them through intricate networks of weights and generators.” The core is “an artificial intelligence powers Chicago’s energy grid.” The singular head intelligence controls the main verb; nearby plural energies, weights and generators do not.

  • A. Have been powering has a plural finite auxiliary that does not agree with intelligence.
  • B. Power is the plural/base present form and does not agree with the singular subject.
  • C. Powers agrees with singular intelligence.
  • D. Are powering has a plural finite auxiliary that does not agree with intelligence.

Question 10 · Difficult

In her 1983 book The Managed Heart: Commercialization of Human Feeling, sociologist Arlie Russell Hochschild first explored at length her conception of a “sociology of emotions”—the idea that the various cultural and ideological frameworks a person has internalized (class, gender, political affiliation, etc.) ______ each emotional reaction that person has within a situation.

Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?

Incorrect · Correct answer: C

Show explanation

Inside the content clause beginning “that,” the core is “the various cultural and ideological frameworks underlie each emotional reaction.” The object-relative clause “a person has internalized” modifies frameworks and has omitted that. Has internalized belongs to person; the main verb in the content clause agrees with plural frameworks.

  • A. Underlies is singular and does not agree with frameworks.
  • B. Is underlying has a singular finite auxiliary.
  • C. Underlie agrees with plural frameworks.
  • D. Has been underlying has a singular finite auxiliary.
