Question 1

Number of Infrared Beam Breaks by Mice Treated with CNO or Saline, by Sex

To investigate the influence of certain estrogen-responsive neurons on energy expenditure, biologist Stephanie Correa et al. treated female and male mice with either saline solution or clozapine-N4-oxide (CNO), which activates the neurons. Monitoring the activity levels of the mice by measuring how frequently the animals broke infrared beams crossing their enclosures, Correa et al. found that the mice in their study showed sex-specific differences in response to neuron activation: ______

Which choice most effectively uses data from the graph to complete the assertion?

  1. the four groups of mice differed greatly in their activity levels before treatment but showed identical activity levels at the end of the monitoring period.
  2. saline-treated females showed substantially more activity at certain points in the monitoring period than saline-treated males did.
  3. CNO-treated females showed more activity relative to saline-treated females than CNO-treated males showed relative to saline-treated males.
  4. CNO-treated females showed a substantial increase and then decline in activity over the monitoring period, whereas CNO-treated males showed a substantial decline in activity followed by a steep increase.
Worked Answer

C. CNO-treated females showed more activity relative to saline-treated females than CNO-treated males showed relative to saline-treated males.

  1. The assertion to complete: female and male mice responded differently to neuron activation. CNO activates the neurons and saline does not, so a response is the CNO group set against the saline group of the same sex.
  2. Females: the CNO line climbs to about 1,400 while the saline line drops below 500. Males: the CNO line stays near 500 while the saline line drops. The gap is much larger for females.
  3. A gap that differs between the sexes is a sex-specific difference in response.
Method from Question 1
  • Read the title, both axes and the key first, so that each line has a name.
  • Read the sentence with the blank and say what the data would have to show.
  • Decide which lines the claim is about: a response to a treatment is the treated group compared with its own untreated group.
  • Test each choice twice: is it true of the graph, and does it show what the sentence needs?
