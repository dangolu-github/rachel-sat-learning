The Comma (continued)

  • Find the real verb. A finite verb shows tense (acts, acted), and in the present it changes with its subject (moss acts → mosses act). An -ing word, an -ed word in a phrase and to + verb never change, so they are never the verb of a clause.
  • to is never the verb. to + noun is a prepositional phrase; to + verb gives a purpose.
  • ✓ In winter, moss acts as an insulator, protecting the roots. (acts = verb; protecting = phrase)
  • ✓ Scholars trace the school to the circles of Kyoto. (to + noun)
  • ✓ Its thinkers merged two traditions to forge a new philosophy. (to + verb: purpose)
  • Pattern: a phrase can open the second sentence (Comma homework 6 and 10). If the words after the blank begin with a phrase and a comma, keep reading. A subject + finite verb after that phrase means a new IC: period or semicolon, not a comma.

  • -ing or -ed? Ask who does the action. -ing: the noun does it (active). -ed: the noun receives it (passive: something done by someone). The -ed word is not the past tense of the sentence.
  • ✗ The bridge, building by my grandpa, was 10 m long.
  • ✓ The bridge, built by my grandpa, was 10 m long. (the bridge was built)
  • ✓ My grandpa, building the bridge, worked every weekend. (grandpa builds)
  • A noun with a that / which / who clause is still one phrase. In Question 6, helped belongs to that. Cross the clause out and a finding is left: a noun phrase, so one comma before it.
  • Name + title or title + name. Either can come first; the part that renames the other is extra and takes a comma at both ends.
  • ✓ The probe sent back 900 images, a result that surprised its designers.
  • ✓ Jane Okafor, a biologist at the institute, led the trial.
  • ✓ A biologist at the institute, Jane Okafor, led the trial.

Homework Corrections

  • Comma 6 · C (1920s. During): during … is only a phrase; its thinkers merged is a second IC.
  • Comma 8 · A (fluctuations, whereas): IC + whereas clause: comma before whereas, none after.
  • Comma 9 · B (however. It): Two ICs (was performed, has been revived); however stays with the first one.
  • Comma 10 · C (Négritude. Both): it would later inspire is a second IC.
  • Agreement 4 · B (enable): The subject is in the blank’s own sentence: tools.
  • Agreement 7 · B (distributes): An -ing action as the subject is singular.
    Encircling the city with roads distributes traffic.
  • Agreement 9 · B (provoked): A and B is plural; A together with B matches A only. in the 1990s → simple past.
    The coach and her players were late. · The coach, together with her players, was late.

Review Tips

  • Boundaries: Read to the end on both sides of the blank. Cross out phrases, then find each subject + finite verb. Two ICs: period, semicolon, or comma + FANBOYS.
  • Real verb: It shows tense, and in the present it changes when the subject becomes plural. -ing, -ed in a phrase and to + verb never do.
  • Agreement: and joins subjects (plural); together with, along with and as well as do not.
  • Homework: Comma 10 and Agreement 10. Mark the verbs before you look at the choices.
