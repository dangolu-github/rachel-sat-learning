Settle the number first, then let the time words choose the tense: “in the 1990s” needs the simple past.

  • A. Singular “has” with the plural subject “theories”; “has provoked” also cannot go with a finished past time (“in the 1990s”).
  • B. Correct: “provoked” fits the plural subject (a past-tense verb looks the same for one or many) and the finished past time.
  • C. Right number, wrong time: present “provoke” cannot describe the 1990s.
  • D. Singular “was” with the plural subject “theories.”
