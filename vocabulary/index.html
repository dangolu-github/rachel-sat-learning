Your vocabulary route

The themes below will connect to your reading lessons. The first unit will focus on evidence and research. No vocabulary exercises are available yet.

Coming first

Evidence and research

Language for claims, findings, uncertainty, and the strength of evidence.

Connects to Claim and Evidence and scientific passages.

Later

Relationships and change

Language for contrast, cause, degree, and changes over time.

Connects to Text Structure and Purpose and Cross-Text Connections.

Later

Literary attitudes and tone

Language for motivation, perspective, and shades of attitude.

Connects to literary passages and poetry.

In each unit

Explore words in context, distinguish close meanings, recall them without a definition, then apply them to a new passage. Return later for a short review.

Vocabulary learning supports Words in Context, which has its own question-type practice.
